        # Export inference-time variance adaptor predictions for later plotting.
        variance_dir = os.path.join(train_config["path"]["result_path"], "variance_raw")

        for i, sample_id in enumerate(batch[0]):
            valid_len = int(batch[4][i].item()) if hasattr(batch[4][i], "item") else int(batch[4][i])
            np.savez_compressed(
                os.path.join(variance_dir, f"{sample_id}.npz"),
                pitch=output[2][i, :valid_len].detach().cpu().numpy(),
                energy=output[3][i, :valid_len].detach().cpu().numpy(),
                log_duration=output[4][i, :valid_len].detach().cpu().numpy(),
                duration=output[5][i, :valid_len].detach().cpu().numpy(),
            )

Then call `scripts/plot_variance.py` for each exported NPZ and place the resulting duration, pitch and energy figures under the matching language/sample folders.

Important: this hook only works after a trained checkpoint exists. It exports predictions; it does not create or train a model.
